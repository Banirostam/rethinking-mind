# Key Questions — Phase I

## Core Conceptual Questions

- What counts as the explanandum in theories of mind?
- Do different theories of mind actually answer the same question?

## Epistemological Questions

- How can stable and universal knowledge arise from variable experience?
- What is the role of perception in the formation of knowledge?

## Ontological Questions

- Is cognition fundamentally abstract or embodied?
- Can cognition be fully explained through living organization?

## Methodological Questions

- How do explanatory priorities shape theories of mind?
- When do two theories genuinely conflict, and when do they address different problems?

## Relevance to AI

- What exactly do AI systems explain: performance, representation, or understanding?
- Are current limitations in AI technical, or conceptual?

## Transition Questions (for next phases)

- How does the tension between abstraction and embodiment evolve in later philosophy?
- What remains unresolved after Plato and Aristotle?
