# Phase I — Classical Foundations

## What this phase really does

This phase does not simply compare Plato and Aristotle.
It introduces a shift in how theories of mind are understood.

Instead of asking:
“What is the mind?”

It asks:
“What is being explained when we talk about the mind?”

## Core Idea

The history of mind is not a sequence of competing answers,
but a sequence of changing explanatory priorities.

## Why it matters

Modern AI inherits these tensions without resolving them.
